Relay Rider Moto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

In short: Relay Rider Moto collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress is saved on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Data controller

Cosmic Production d.o.o.
Split, Croatia
Email: ivan@cosmicproduction.hr

What the game stores, and where

Your progress (cleared routes, medals, best times, local ghosts, Bolts earned and spent, equipped bikes and cosmetics) and your settings are saved in the app's protected data area on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The game has no server of its own, no cloud save, no public leaderboard, no chat and no player uploads. Daily and weekly routes are chosen on your device from the routes already in the app.

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking

Relay Rider Moto has no user account. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or advertising components and no third-party SDK. It reads no advertising identifier, builds no usage profile and never shows an App Tracking Transparency request.

Purchases

The game offers optional one-time in-app purchases (the Full Campaign and three cosmetic packs). The purchase and the payment are processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's terms and privacy policy; Apple receives the purchase data it needs to do this. We receive no payment details, card data or Apple ID. On your device, the game asks Apple's StoreKit which items you own so it can unlock them, including when you tap Restore Purchases.

Sharing a result card

If you choose to share a result, the game creates an image of that result (course, bike appearance, time and mode) and opens the system share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick a destination yourself, and the game never shares automatically or contacts anyone for you.

Legal basis and retention

Because we neither collect nor process personal data, no processing by us takes place within the meaning of the GDPR. The data on your device stays there until you delete the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing we can disclose or erase on request; control over your game data rests with you, on your device. For purchase data held by Apple, see Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example by adding an optional online feature), this policy is updated before that version is released, and the store listing's privacy details are updated with it.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to ivan@cosmicproduction.hr.
