How the game is sold

  • Free introduction: the first 12 routes are free, with no time limit, no ads and no account.
  • Full Campaign: one optional one-time purchase adds the other 36 routes (worlds 2 to 4). The routes open through normal progression after purchase. Bikes are still earned by clearing their milestones.
  • Cosmetic packs: three optional one-time packs (Signal, After Hours, Supporter) change looks only. They do not open routes or make a bike faster.
  • Bolts are earned by riding and cannot be bought.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device or after reinstalling?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the Shop (or tap a locked world) and tap "Restore Purchases". Your Full Campaign and cosmetic packs come back without a new charge. Restoring needs an internet connection; after that, owned content plays offline.
I bought the Full Campaign but a world is still closed.
The purchase gives ownership of worlds 2 to 4; each world then opens through normal progression on the map. If the map still shows the world as not owned, tap "Restore Purchases". If a purchase says it is waiting for approval (for example Ask to Buy), keep riding the free routes; it unlocks when approved.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Riding, progress and daily routes work offline. Buying and restoring need a connection to the App Store.
Is there cloud save, an online leaderboard or cross-platform progress?
Not in this version. Progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the app deletes it. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored.
The game is not working correctly. What should I do?
Close and reopen the game and make sure you have the latest version. If the problem persists, email us a short description of what you did and what happened.
